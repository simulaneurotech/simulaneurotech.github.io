        How this was made

        Co-authors and their institutions come from OpenAlex, looked up by DOI for the investigators' publications in NVA (Nasjonalt vitenarkiv). Publications without a DOI, mostly older ones, are not included. Collaborating institutions are the co-authors' institutions. Members of the Simula Group, and the investigators' own other affiliations on their papers (such as a professorship at a university), are not counted.

        Projects come from Simula's project pages and the NVA project register, selected and checked by hand. Funders are shown as listed on the project pages.
