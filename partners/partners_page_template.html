        How this was made

        Co-authors and their institutions come from OpenAlex, looked up by DOI for the investigators' publications in NVA (Nasjonalt vitenarkiv). Publications without a DOI, mostly older ones, are not included. Each co-author is counted once, at their main institution: the one they are listed at most often on these publications in the period, as on the overview page. Colleagues whose main institution is in the Simula Group, and the investigators' own other affiliations on their papers (such as a professorship at a university), are not counted.

        Research areas come from the name of each co-author's department, from the first of these that names one: their affiliation on the publications with the centre's investigators; their public ORCID record; their affiliation on their other recent publications; at research institutes and companies, the field of the institution; an institute, centre or laboratory; and, as an uncertain classification, a faculty covering several areas or the field of their research in OpenAlex. At hospitals, a department decides, and otherwise clinical medicine. Departments are counted per institution, and an institution taken as a whole (such as a company) counts as one. The area is that of the department a person belongs to, not always their research: a physicist working on the brain counts as physics. A few institutions and departments are set by hand where OpenAlex has none or the wrong one.

        Projects come from Simula's project pages and the NVA project register, selected and checked by hand. Funders are shown as listed on the project pages.
