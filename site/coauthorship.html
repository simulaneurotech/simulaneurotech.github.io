What stands out

    How this was made

    Each investigator's publications were fetched from the API of NVA, which replaced Cristin, on 30 October 2026, using the profile with a Simula affiliation (and a second profile where one exists). Duplicate records of the same paper, including preprints and their published versions, are counted once. Two investigators share a publication when both are among its authors, recognised by Cristin ID or, where the ID is missing, by name. Some Cristin records list a book's editors as authors of every chapter; editors are removed from chapters they did not write. Outside co-authors are people outside the centre on the investigators' publications, identified by Cristin ID or, mostly for authors abroad, by name.

    NVA, like Cristin before it, only holds work registered through Norwegian institutions, so work from before someone joined Simula, or from abroad, may be missing. A missing line means no shared publication in NVA, not necessarily no collaboration. 2026 covers January to October only.
