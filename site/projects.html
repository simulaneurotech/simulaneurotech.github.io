How this was made

The projects are those that started in 2016 or later and are led by one of the centre's investigators, or where an investigator leads Simula's part of a project led elsewhere. They come from Simula's project pages, the NVA project register, Prosjektbanken (the Research Council of Norway's project database) and CORDIS (the EU's), and are selected and checked by hand. Where the sources give different dates, the funder's are used.

Leaders and participants are the centre's investigators; other people on the projects are not listed. Collaborators are the institutions that these sources, or the funder, list for each project, and the source is given with each project. Bars are coloured by the project's main funder. The logos are the projects' and funders' own.
