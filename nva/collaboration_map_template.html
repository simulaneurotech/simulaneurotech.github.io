How this was made

Publications are the investigators' records in NVA (Nasjonalt vitenarkiv), with duplicates, such as a preprint and its published version, counted once. Two investigators share a publication when both are among its authors. Outside co-authors are the other authors of the investigators' publications.

NVA mainly holds work registered through Norwegian institutions, so work from before someone joined Simula may be missing. A missing line means no shared publication in NVA, not necessarily no collaboration. The current year is not yet complete.
