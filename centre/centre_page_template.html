    How this was made

    Publications are the investigators' journal articles, conference papers, book chapters and books in NVA (Nasjonalt vitenarkiv), with duplicates merged and each publication counted once however many investigators are on it.

    Co-authors are everyone on these publications other than the centre's investigators, from OpenAlex, which covers the publications with a DOI. Each co-author is counted once, at their main institution: the one they are listed at most often on these publications. Colleagues whose main institution is in the Simula Group, and the Simula Group institutions themselves, are not counted.
